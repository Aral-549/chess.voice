# Recording script - VoiceChessmate launch film

Record against `assets/video/for-voiceover/launch-film-SILENT.mp4`.
That file is 164.000s with **no audio track**, so your voice sits on its own.
A music bed at 18 percent is in `launch-film-MUSIC-QUIET.mp4` if you want one
under the voice later.

Read straight through. The gaps are built into the timings, so you do not need
to stop and restart between scenes.

Every line below is paced for 140-175 words per minute, which is comfortable
conversational reading. Six scenes were trimmed to get there; the originals ran
up to 246 wpm, which cannot be read aloud. Where a line was cut, the note says
so, and the argument it was making survives in the line that remains.

Tone: explaining something you care about to one person. Not a product
announcement. Let the silences sit.

## If you fluff a line

Record that scene again on its own and drop it in at its timecode. Every scene
starts and ends on a cut, so a replacement does not need to match the take
either side.

## What not to say

No latency number. None has been measured, and it is the one claim a judge can
check and disprove. If you want one, press **L** while you play and read what
the app tells you.
